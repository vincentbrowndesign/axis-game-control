## Central question

Why does recorded basketball footage so rarely become useful basketball information?

## Trophy Labs point of view

Film is only valuable when a coach can return to the moments that mattered without rewatching the whole session. Capture quality decides how much of that is possible, and it is decided before any analysis runs.

## Claim ledger
