# Alt text - Why your basketball film isn't telling you anything

Instagram takes alt text per image. Set it while adding each slide to the carousel,
in this order. Write nothing new in the app - paste these.

## Slide 01 - HOOK

Slide 1 of 8. Large white condensed text on a near-black background reading: Your camera roll isn't a film lab. An orange bar sits under the headline.

## Slide 02 - PROBLEM

Slide 2 of 8. An orange label reads The problem. White text reads: You recorded 40 minutes of basketball. Now somebody has to watch all 40 minutes again.

## Slide 03 - FILM_FRAME

Slide 3 of 8. A strip of seven empty film frames runs across the slide with a single frame outlined in orange. Text reads: The useful moments are buried between setup, rebounds, walking, resets, and dead time.

## Slide 04 - BIG_STATEMENT

Slide 4 of 8. An orange label reads Coaching reality. Large white text reads: In the gym, the coach's job is to coach. Smaller grey text underneath reads: Not stop after every rep to organize video.

## Slide 05 - SEQUENCE

Slide 5 of 8. An orange label reads A better operating model. A numbered sequence lists four steps: one, record. Two, add film. Three, keep coaching. Four, return to the moments.

## Slide 06 - QUOTE

Slide 6 of 8. Large white text reads: A useful basketball system should show what the film supports, and hold back when it can't tell. Attributed to a Trophy Labs operating principle.

## Slide 07 - CHECKLIST

Slide 7 of 8. An orange label reads Better film starts before analysis. A checklist with orange marks lists: stable camera, bright view, player visible, ball visible, hoop visible when possible.
