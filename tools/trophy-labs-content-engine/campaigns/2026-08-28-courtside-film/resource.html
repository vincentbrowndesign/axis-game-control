Courtside Film Setup Checklist

Capture footage that is easier to break down.

A one-page setup pass before you press record. Nothing here depends on Trophy Labs - it makes any basketball film easier to review.

  • Put the camera behind or diagonally beside the shooter
  • Keep the player's full body in frame
  • Include the hoop when possible
  • Use a stable tripod or fixed surface
  • Avoid shooting directly into bright lights
  • Prefer a bright indoor court for the current V1
  • Keep the ball visible at release and during flight
  • Avoid frequent zooming or camera movement
  • Record complete reps instead of stopping immediately after release
  • Upload the original-quality file when practical
